Why “US”

Strength grows in connection.

United Strengths exists for one reason: no one facing drug or alcohol addiction should believe they have to face it alone. US means all of us coming together, carrying hope when someone else is tired. This community is for people still struggling with drugs or alcohol, people who have survived addiction and are living in recovery, and the people who love them. Help is available whether you are thinking about change, trying again, or years into recovery. Reach for a person, a meeting, a helpline, or professional care whenever you need it.

There is no single perfect recovery story. You belong here while you’re figuring yours out. Prayer is welcome, and so is every person who needs support, whatever their beliefs.

“We are stronger together. When one of us needs lifting, the rest of US reach back.”

From the founder

Why I started United Strengths

My name is Templar, and I know what it means to struggle with drug addiction. I used crack, cocaine, meth, and Ecstasy. Today, I have been clean for 14 years.

I share that because I want someone who is still struggling to see a real person on the other side of it. I cannot tell you that everyone’s path will look like mine. I can tell you that my life did not end with addiction, and yours does not have to either.

If you are hurting, starting over, or wondering whether change is possible, please hear me: you are not alone. There are people who will listen, meetings you can walk into, and professionals who can help. Reaching out can be your first step.

United Strengths is my way of reaching back. I want us to be a community that meets people with love, care, compassion, support, and prayer when they want it. I hope you find a little hope here, then carry it with you into the next step.

— Templar
Founder, United Strengths
